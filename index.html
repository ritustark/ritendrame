01 — Who I Am

A founder's mind ready to serve the ecosystem

My journey into startups began during college with GreenCycle—a hydrogen-energy project that sparked my obsession with how emerging technologies get built and scaled in the real world.

Since then I've co-founded Evolsyn, shipped products to thousands of users, hired teams, navigated pilots in international markets, and learned firsthand what separates ideas that stick from those that don't.
